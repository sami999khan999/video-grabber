Video Grabber

The extension sends links to your Video Grabber server and shows its downloads.

Quick picks:

Where the container listens. The default 7845 is rarely used by other software; 8080, 8000 and 8888 are common choices but often taken by other dev tools. Another computer works too, e.g. http://192.168.1.20:7845. Don't have one running? Start it with: 



        
        

        

          
          
        

      

    


    

      YouTube login
      

        Your history, feeds, private playlists and YouTube's "sign in to confirm you're not a
        bot" check need your YouTube login on the server. The extension hands it over by itself
        from this browser, no cookies.txt export needed: before every YouTube lookup, every 6 hours,
        and a minute after you log in or out. Only youtube.com cookies are read, and the server only
        uses them when YouTube insists. A spare account is safest.

      Any website
      

        The server knows about 1,800 video sites by name. These two switches let the extension
        grab from almost any other site too. Both are off until you turn them on.
      


      
      

        The extension notices the video files and streams (MP4, HLS, DASH) a page plays and lists
        them in the popup under Playing on this page. Your browser will ask to allow access
        to all sites; nothing is sent anywhere until you press Grab.
      


      
      

        For videos that only play while you're logged in. Your cookies for that one site go to

        they're never stored or shown. Only turn this on if the server is your own, because
        whoever runs it could use them.
